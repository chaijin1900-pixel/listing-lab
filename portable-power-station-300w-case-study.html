Title

Portable Power Station 300W, 296Wh Solar Generator with AC/USB-C/DC Output Ports, Quiet Backup Battery for Camping, Home Backup & CPAP, Recharge by Solar/Wall/Car, UL94 V-0 Flame-Retardant Shell

Bullet Points

  • CAPACITY: 296Wh lithium battery powers phones up to 24 times, a 45W CPAP for about 5 to 6 hours, a 60W mini fridge for about 4 hours, or LED camp lights for multiple nights.
  • VERSATILE OUTPUT: 300W pure sine wave AC outlet with 600W surge, 60W USB-C PD, 2 USB-A ports, 12V/10A DC car port, and 2 DC5521 ports for laptops, routers, cameras, drones, and small appliances.
  • FAST RECHARGE: Recharge from 0-80% in about 2.5 hours by wall adapter, 4 to 5 hours from a 100W solar panel in full sun, or 3.5 hours from a 12V car outlet while driving.
  • SAFE & SILENT: Battery management system protects against over-voltage, over-current, short circuit, and overheating, while the fanless low-load mode keeps noise below 30 dB for tents, bedrooms, and CPAP use.
  • WHAT'S INCLUDED: Portable power station, AC wall adapter, car charging cable, DC5521 cable, user manual, and 24-month support; solar panel sold separately.

Description

Built for camping weekends, storm outages, road trips, and CPAP backup, this 300W portable power station combines a 296Wh battery with AC, USB-C, USB-A, DC, and car-socket outputs so shoppers can run the everyday essentials they actually search for.

The pure sine wave AC output helps protect sensitive electronics, while multi-layer BMS protection and a UL94 V-0 flame-retardant shell address common safety concerns. At light loads, the quiet operating profile stays suitable for sleeping areas, tents, RV bunks, and bedside medical-device backup.

Flexible solar, wall, and car recharge options reduce downtime: top up quickly before a trip, recover from a wall outlet after an outage, or connect a compatible 100W solar panel during daylight. The result is a compact solar generator for camping, home backup, CPAP power, phones, laptops, cameras, and emergency kits.
