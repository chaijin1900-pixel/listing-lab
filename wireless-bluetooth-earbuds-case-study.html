AFTER

Title

Wireless Bluetooth Earbuds with 10mm Drivers, ANC & Transparency Mode, Bluetooth 5.3 Ear Buds, 8Hr Earbuds + 32Hr USB-C Charging Case, IPX5 Sweat Resistant Headphones for Gym, Commute & Remote Work

Bullet Points

  • SOUND QUALITY: 10mm dynamic drivers deliver balanced bass, clear vocals, and crisp podcasts with AAC/SBC codec support, so playlists, training videos, and calls sound full without overpowering mids.
  • ANC & TRANSPARENCY: Hybrid active noise cancellation reduces steady background noise by up to 28 dB for commuting and office focus, while transparency mode lets in voices and traffic when you need awareness.
  • BATTERY LIFE: Listen for up to 8 hours per charge with ANC off or about 6 hours with ANC on; the compact USB-C case extends total listening time to 32 hours and supports a 10-minute quick charge for about 90 minutes of playback.
  • SECURE FIT: Lightweight 4.6 g earbuds include 3 sizes of silicone ear tips and an IPX5 sweat-resistant build for gym sessions, walking commutes, video calls, and everyday remote-work use.
  • WHAT'S INCLUDED: Wireless Bluetooth earbuds, USB-C charging case, USB-C cable, S/M/L silicone ear tips, quick-start guide, and 12-month support; works with iPhone, Android, tablets, and Bluetooth-enabled laptops.

Description

Built around 10mm dynamic drivers, these wireless Bluetooth earbuds are tuned for practical everyday listening: clean vocals for podcasts and calls, enough low-end punch for workouts, and stable Bluetooth 5.3 pairing for phones, tablets, and laptops. The result is a more specific, searchable audio listing that speaks to gym, commute, and remote-work shoppers.

Hybrid ANC helps lower consistent distractions like train noise, fans, and office hum, while transparency mode keeps conversations and street sounds accessible without removing the earbuds. Dual microphones with environmental noise reduction improve voice pickup for Zoom meetings, phone calls, and voice notes in moderately noisy spaces.

Each earbud runs up to 8 hours with ANC off, and the USB-C charging case extends total listening time to 32 hours before the case needs a recharge. A 10-minute quick charge provides about 90 minutes of playback, and the IPX5 sweat-resistant design handles workouts, light rain, and daily carry without overpromising full waterproof protection.
